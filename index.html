Simple nutrition rules

  • Spread protein across 3–5 meals.
  • Build meals around lean protein, vegetables or fruit, high-fiber carbs, and healthy fats.
  • Drink water regularly; adjust for heat and sweat.
  • Weigh in several mornings per week and compare weekly averages.
  • After 2–3 weeks, if average weight is not trending down, reduce by 150–200 kcal; if losing too quickly or strength is dropping, add a little food.
Your calorie needs are an estimate. The trend over several weeks—not a single weigh-in—tells you whether to adjust.

Creatine

Simple daily habit

5 g

Creatine monohydrate

Every day, including rest days

Best timing: whenever you will remember—breakfast, with a meal, or after training. Consistency matters more than the exact time.

Daily 3–5 g is the better-supported maintenance routine. No loading phase is required. A small increase in water weight can happen. If you have kidney disease or concerns about medications, check with a healthcare professional first.
